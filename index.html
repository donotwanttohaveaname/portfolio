03how b2b buying works now

most of the deal
happens before
anyone fills
in a form.

B2B funnels stopped being funnels. Buyers loop, lurk, ask their peers, and put a shortlist together long before they are ready to talk. The work moved earlier, into the part you never see in the CRM.

86%

already have a vendor list on day one, before researching properly.

72%

start by asking a peer, not a search engine or a salesperson.

So the job is being on that list before the shopping starts. Here's how I'd go about it.
"we'll look at it next quarter" "who else did you shortlist?" "never heard of them"
95%

of deals go to vendors already on the buyer's day-1 shortlist

Bain, 6sense, LinkedIn B2B Institute and Gartner buyer studies, 2024–2026
04how i work

get on the list,
in four steps

We agree the direction early, so building can start almost straight away. Then it loops, because step four feeds straight back into step one.

01

define

Messaging, audience, ICP. Who we're talking to, what problem we solve, and why anyone should care. Everything downstream gets easier once this one is settled.

02

build

Funnel design from problem-aware to product-aware. Lead magnets, content, ad concepts, nurture sequences, the pages they land on. The part where things actually get made.

03

distribute

Find where buyers search and spend time. LinkedIn, Google, Meta, AI search, your own list. Pick the channels, launch the campaigns, reach actual humans.

04

improve

Measure, iterate, experiment. Influenced pipeline, channel metrics, what's working and what isn't. The step that never ends, in a good way.
