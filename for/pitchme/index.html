01where you are today

a strong story, an empty ad lane

I looked at pitchme.co and LinkedIn's Ad Library before writing this. Here's what I found.

linkedin ads

No LinkedIn ads from PitchMe

Every bit of reach you get on LinkedIn today is organic. That means it's unpredictable: the same good post can reach thousands of recruitment leaders one week and forty people the next.

Paid reach is how you make sure the right recruitment firms see your people every week, whatever the algorithm decides.

buyers

You sell to recruitment firms

Your site calls PitchMe "the enterprise intelligence layer for high-growth recruitment firms", and your customer stories come from staffing and recruitment businesses.

Recruiters live on LinkedIn. It's their sourcing tool, their inbox and their newsfeed. Your buyers don't need to be pulled onto a new channel.

proof

Customer results that make great posts

Your customers report 3.2x ROI in six months, 40% higher fill rates and 50% faster time-to-hire. "Your next hire is already in your database" is a hook recruiters will feel.

These are stories, and stories told by a person get read. On a banner they get scrolled past.

the good news

You already have loud voices

Your founder and team already post and speak about recruitment data. That's the hardest part of thought leadership ads, and you have it.

We don't need to invent a voice. We take the people recruiters already listen to and make sure the right ones hear them.

02why thought leadership ads

seven reasons this is the right format for you

A thought leadership ad is a real post from a real person at your company, promoted as an ad. It looks and reads like any other post in the feed, and it reaches the people you choose. These reasons come from running them every week for B2B SaaS companies.

1

people trust people

Buyers follow and believe individuals. A post from someone with a face and a name gets read; a company logo gets skipped.

2

organic is a lottery

A good post can reach thousands or forty people, and you can't predict which. Paid reach makes sure the right recruitment leaders actually see it.

3

you pick who sees it

Target by company, industry, job title and seniority: owners, heads of delivery, recruitment ops, COOs at staffing and recruitment firms. No wasted reach.

4

it feels native

It sits in the feed like any other post and works on mobile, where recruiters spend their day. No banner blindness.

5

cheaper attention

Across the B2B accounts I manage, thought leadership ads usually cost around half as much per click as standard single-image ads.Engagement rates of 6 to 10% are common on good ones.

6

it builds an audience

Everyone who engages becomes an audience we can retarget with a direct product and demo message. Awareness turns into something you can convert.

7

it lifts your people too

Every ad grows the person behind it. I've seen a founder with almost no LinkedIn presence pass 100 likes on a single promoted post.
