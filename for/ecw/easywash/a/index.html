FAQ

Questions operators ask first.

What is EasyWash?

EasyWash is a car wash subscription and payment platform made by Euro Car Wash in Riihimäki, Finland. It covers the driver app, the payment terminal, licence plate recognition, fleet invoicing and the operator dashboard. More than 700 wash sites run on it.

Do we have to replace our payment terminals?

No. You can launch memberships with the app and a camera per lane and keep the terminals you have. Add EasyWash terminals later, when a unit is due for replacement.

Which wash machines does it work with?

Christ, Sonny's, PDQ, Kärcher and other brands, on rollovers, tunnels and self-service jet wash bays. EasyWash also controls doors, booms and dryers.

Can a member share a plan with friends?

A plan is tied to one licence plate, and the camera checks the plate at every visit. Each car needs its own plan.

How long does installation take?

At Divi Vienā in Latvia, each rollover station took three to four hours and was in use the same day. Larger rollouts follow the same four stages, site by site.

What does it cost?

The price depends on the number of sites and the parts you take. You get a price for your setup on the first call.

Is there an app for drivers?

Yes. Drivers find EasyWash stations and manage their plan at easywash.com. This page is for the businesses that run the washes.
