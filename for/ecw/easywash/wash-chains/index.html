Getting started

Live in four stages. We do the work.

  1. AgreementOne contract for software, hardware and support.
  2. TrainingYour staff learn the dashboard and the questions drivers ask.
  3. ConfigurationWe set up wash programmes, prices, plans, limits and user terms with you.
  4. InstallationTrained installers fit the hardware, connect it to your machine and test it. At Divi Vienā each station took three to four hours.
