How a subscription wash works

From the driver's side there is nothing to learn. From the operator's side there is nothing to manage by hand.

Driver buys a membership

At the terminal, in the white-label app or on the web. Monthly, annual or pay-per-wash packages, in the local language and currency.

Terminal recognises the plate

The built-in camera reads the licence plate at the bay. No card, code or app needed from then on.

Wash starts

EasyWash starts the programme on the wash machine and controls doors, dryers and other external devices.

Revenue arrives every month

Subscriptions are billed automatically. Fleet customers get invoices with correct VAT. You see usage and revenue per site in real time.

Questions operators ask first

What is EasyWash?

EasyWash is an unattended car wash payment and membership system made by Euro Car Wash in Riihimäki, Finland. A terminal at the wash bay recognises the licence plate, takes contactless payment or starts a subscription wash, and reports revenue per site.

Does it work with my existing wash machine?

Yes. The terminal controls the wash machine and external devices and runs with Christ, Sonny's, PDQ, Kärcher and other brands. There is no Washtec contract or ecosystem dependency.

How do subscriptions work, and what do they bring in?

Operators sell monthly, annual or pay-per-wash packages at the terminal, in the app or on the web. The member's plate is recognised, the wash starts and the subscription is billed every month. OKQ8 runs 100,000+ active subscriptions on EasyWash, about €3 million a month in recurring wash revenue.

Which countries and languages do you support?

EasyWash runs at 700+ locations in Finland, Sweden, Norway, the Baltics, Poland and Germany. The terminal, app, dashboard and support are available in Finnish, English, Swedish, Norwegian, German, Polish, Lithuanian and Latvian.

How do we start without a full rollout?

Most networks start with a 90-day pilot on their highest-traffic site, then roll out. The terminal works with the equipment you already have, so a pilot does not require new machines.
